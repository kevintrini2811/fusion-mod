# Magnet Blover port (24.1.1)

The only source recipe is Blover + Magnet-shroom, accepted in both orders. The persisted identity is native Magnetshroom with marker Blover. Source prefab42478 supplies the complete body and idle/blow/blowloop clips. The source contract and raw methods are linked in [the shared report](../magnet-pair-contract/report.md).

The family owns a canonical initial50cs digestion countdown, then100cs per captured item. Its wind event occurs at39cs (the first100Hz boundary after the source0.383333325s animation event), calls the existing balloon/fog owner, and starts the1000cs lifetime. There is no independent ten-item cap. The original radius is40 world units with an explicit maximum row distance of two. Candidate order follows the native Board arrays rather than adding a nearest-target sort.

Native MagnetShroomAttactItem removes actual equipment and creates its saved MagnetItem. The ordinary Magnetshroom uses slot zero; GoldMagnet alone searches five slots. The family reconstructs the original0.3s linear captured-object flight from current position and remaining saved action age. No projectile exists. Native Plant.Draw still calls DrawMagnetItems at0x6af107 independently of the skipped ability body; its factory parents MagnetItemController below the plant controller. Imported body hiding explicitly preserves those equipment controllers. The live test must still verify their visible rendering. Native placed ladders transfer through GridItemDie and the native LadderPlaced representation; the native consumed-ladder semantics remain. Real BucketDrops transfer through their responsible owner, excluding held items and nonmetal Eyes heads. Captured Pails and FootballHelmets reuse the existing bucket selection and fusion transaction. Selecting a captured item does not stop this family’s digestion or lifetime; expiration cancels the selection before another object reuses its slot. Successful use clears physical ownership. Their age pauses while captured. Blover destroys captured physical items on death, matching the source1102 exception. It never synthesizes a second ground drop.

mLaunchCounter owns digestion; mTargetX owns age; mStartRow owns captured-object/action age; mTargetY packs external light and physical-item kind; mLaunchRate holds captured-item age. Generic native Update/UpdateAbilities do not write these counters (see ../magnet-pair-native/); native StateCountdown is cleared after attraction because its generic native decrement is not a free source timer. One FusionTime.SourceStep occurs per family owner and full entity generation, inside the existing UpdateMagnetShroom call and Zombie Library armor-borrow scope. The state survives ordinary native serialization without a parallel clock record.

Fertilizer heals through the existing owner and caps positive digestion at50cs. It does not alter wind or lifetime. Native mushroom, coffee and terrain rules are retained as an explicit platform adaptation. The source bucket offer has no available recipient: Hypno Magnet, ultimate hypno and MagnetBox consumers are not implemented. This port does not introduce a speculative MagnetSystem.

Original asset export, build, focused offline checks and live functional/visual cases are accepted. The final release adds native-binding controller metal pickup and cancellation. See the [pair acceptance record](../magnet-pair-24-1-1/acceptance.md) for exact versions, evidence and limits.
