Real loose BucketDrops are transferred atomically through their owner. Saved native counters retain their physical kind and paused age. The initial attribute countdown is zero. Fertilizer heals and caps a positive countdown at50cs; it does not add light or cause an immediate shock. ## Review corrections,17 November2026

The pinned source searches Door/Ladder second armor, not the unmapped Replanted Garbage shield. It adds no head-presence filter; both restrictions are now corrected and covered by regressions.

`AttrackItem` passes4 as the damage type. The value12 in ECX selects the virtual slot in the IL2CPP invoker at0x2de0; it is not DamageType.Carred. The source Zombie.TakeDamage signature and DamageType enum establish Shieldless4. The port therefore uses native DamageFlags.BypassesShield1. It leaves neighboring second armor intact while normal helmet and body owners process200 damage after the attracted item has been removed. The existing FusionDamageScale final prefix remains the sole converter; pinned ordinary peas are20/20, currently giving a1:1 factor. Isolating tests assert the amount presented to that external boundary, not a new balance rule.

Source Plant.AttackLandZombie at0x466c20 rejects mind control and statuses3,7,9,10,12,16,20,21,22,23,24,44: pole jump, submerged snorkel, dolphin jump, flying, underground miner, airborne imp, high pogo jump, boss and bungee phases. The port now maps those source categories explicitly in ShockEligible instead of delegating to native Ground. Boss and Bungee types are always excluded, including otherwise vulnerable native boss-head and grounded Bungee phases. Pole vault, Dolphin jump, Balloon flight/popping, tunneled Digger (including the post-pickaxe tunneling pause), thrown Imp and submerged Snorkel are excluded. DiggerRising and DiggerRiseWithoutAxe remain eligible, as do landed Imp and native grave/dancer rising transitions. Snorkel uses the native water/eating state to distinguish diving from surfacing. Native TakeDamage remains the final state/armor owner. Source SuperPogo big-jump20 has no current native actor counterpart: source PogoZombie remains Polo_jump14 throughout its ordinary bounces (Awake0x609280 and PositionUpdate0x609c50), while only the separate SuperPogoZombie positions branch on20 (0x635f2d). Native ordinary Pogo high-bounce phases therefore remain eligible. Source-only statuses and actors are not invented in Replanted. The focused mapped-phase regressions execute the real shock path; source-only actors remain unavailable.
