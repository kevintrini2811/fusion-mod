Anchor-shroom (`SeaMagnet`, 1101) uses the native Magnet-shroom with the Sea-shroom marker. Fusion, carrying and debug placement use aquatic terrain. The low-projectile collision scope uses the same path as the existing low sea fusions. `SeaMagnet` calls the base constructor with a four-source-unit search circle, a two-row limit and a 15-second timer, recorded in `rea-r08-bounds-magnet-ctor.json`. `Magnetshroom.AttrackItem` at `0x4ca7d0` starts the attribute timer on acquisition. `SeaMagnet.AttributeEvent` at `0x4e3940` completes the base digestion before advancing the count and switches the interval to ten seconds at three. Replanted uses existing magnet equipment extraction and held-item storage. The saved `mLaunchCounter` holds digestion and `mTargetX` holds the completed count. The existing magnet fertilizer rule caps an active digestion at 50 source ticks. The Lumos Magnet body and portrait are reused. The body has a cool tint and a larger scale from stage three, with the native growth cue.

Orca-shroom (`DoomSeaShroom`, 1303) uses the native Sea-shroom with the Doom-shroom marker. The existing sea-plant feed route consumes a Sea-shroom card or carried plant and keeps the recipient. Its saved `mTargetY` times the feeding animation. The port uses the middle of the reused one-second eat animation as the firing event. `DoomSeaShroom.AnimShoot` at `0x4a5ec0` aims at the nearest grounded zombie ahead in the same row, or eight source units forward. `Lawnf.CalculateProjectileWithSpeed` at `0xa0ef80` confirms a 1.5-second predictive flight. `Bullet_doom_throw.Explode` at `0x704530` applies the small ash blast within two source units, about 1.5 lawn tiles. The source atlas gives 600 base damage. The port commits the blast at landing and uses a Doom projectile view over the shared lobber trajectory. It reuses the Shark-shroom body and portrait with a cool tint. The shot uses a native throw sound and the small blast uses Powie and Chime. Native Sea-shroom sleep and coffee rules apply. Midflight marked shots follow the existing transient Fusion projectile load rule.
