# Squashwood and Torchweed: Fusion 4.0.5.2 port

This port adds exactly two recipe results: Squashwood (1059) and Torchweed (1061). The original Roasted Spikeweed (1155) is a required non-fusible summon, not a third fusion. The installed Replanted `SeedType` enum contains none of these three plants. Original native ingredients remain native. Ingredient availability is recorded in `tools/ingredient-availability.json`.

## Original sources

The authoritative build is `../pvzfusion/english-4.0.5.2/Game Files/`, with GameAssembly SHA256 `48096b917ee6aaf6e35c95c98e666a4399a9e0c3d5adbab727cee941eccf2d42`. The metadata, original Chinese almanac and recipe archive fingerprints accompany the method exports in `../squashwood/research-inputs.json` and `../torchweed/research-inputs.json`. The unofficial expanded source is not the authority for this implementation.

`../squashwood/recipes.json` and `../torchweed/recipes.json` contain every source recipe for these results. Both recipes accept either ingredient order: Squash + Torchwood, and Spikeweed + Torchwood. No unavailable alternative recipe is omitted. Squashwood's original model is SquashTorchPrefab (40367). Torchweed's original model is FireSpikePrefab (49464), using CaltropTorch; it must not be confused with FireCaltropPrefab (64169), the original Roasted Spikeweed (1155). Scorchweed (1062) instead uses JalaSpikePrefab (64162). The official metadata enum defaults, not similarly named translated enum members, establish these IDs. Original mapping and portrait evidence is in `../torchweed-assets-source/`.

## Squashwood

`SquashTorch.OnTriggerEnter2D` (0x4eb450) accepts ordinary peas (source 0) and snow peas (15), subject to the common plant/projectile collision eligibility check. Ordinary peas become yellow fire peas; snow peas become ordinary peas. Both count one conversion. Its comparison is strictly greater than 50. The pinned English almanac incorrectly says 60; the port follows executable behavior and documents this adaptation in both languages.

The threshold invokes virtual `SummonPlant(300)` directly. Official metadata identifies slot 72; the VTable entry is at 0x5b8. This is not an animation callback. The `dmg` argument is not read by the source implementation. `SummonPlant` (0x4eb590) searches from the next column to the right for the first legal placement of source plant 1057, the existing Roasted Squash. Charge resets only after successful placement. A full row retains charge and retries on the next conversion.

`TorchFusions` owns conversion and its saved `mTargetY` charge counter. The existing native projectile flight, yellow fireball graphics and impact remain native. The existing imported Roasted Squash and native Squash lifecycle own the summon attack and graphics.

## Torchweed

`CaltropTorch.OnTriggerEnter2D` (0x496cd0) accepts small peas (10) and rolling Wall-nuts (2). Small peas become small fire peas (50), adding 20 damage. Rolling nuts become fire nuts (232), doubling damage and clearing their hit history. Already ignited nuts are a different source type and are not accepted again. Source camp, lane and producing-plant checks exclude hostile or self-produced projectiles; the port recognizes only its own saved small-pea and rolling-nut families. Torchweed produces neither family itself.

The original comparison is strictly greater than 100; the source resets charge before calling `SummonCaltrop` (0x496eb0). That method searches forward for the first valid placement of source 1155. A full row therefore loses charge. `TorchweedFusions` owns the saved `mTargetX` conversion counter. It uses the shared `TorchPlantIndex`, extended with the Spikeweed native type, rather than scanning the plant population for each projectile. Crossing is swept and marked with the native saved `mHitTorchwoodGridX` field.

Real PeaNut rollers use the existing `RollingProjectiles.Move` motion owner, which bypasses native `CheckForCollision`. That owner invokes Torchweed conversion before the existing ChargeTorch conversion, matching the collision-patch priorities. Both paths share the indexed crossing converter; no second movement sweep was added. Ignition doubles the saved damage and clears prior-hit history once, while the fire marker prevents a later Torchweed from igniting or charging again.

`Update`/`FireUpdate` (0x497380/0x496c70) advance a separate attack countdown (source offset 0x1d0), reset from the attack interval (0x13c). `SummonFire` (0x4970c0) searches a one-world-unit square about the shadow and filters the same lane and hostile camp. The one-unit box is confirmed by executable constant 0x297bb48 = 1.0. It attacks every second for 40 fire damage and removes cold buffs. Vehicle contacts use the original Caltrop vehicle-destruction branch instead of a normal numerical hit. The port calls native ZamboniDeath or CatapultDeath with Spike flags and generation-checked DropLoot, preserving tire/debris animation and health-independent destruction. It completes other contacts in the same source pulse before removing the plant. Native routing evidence is archived in `source-review/native-Plant-DoRowAreaDamage.asm` and `source-review/native-Zombie-TakeBodyDamage.asm`. The ordinary source Balloon has 270 body health plus 20 first-armor health. `ApplyDamage` (0x64e280) dispatches first-armor damage at slot 68; `Balloon.FirstArmorTakeDamage` (0x5c0020) pops its balloon through slot 79 when the 40-damage hit reaches that 20-health armor, returning zero body overflow. Replanted stores flying armor in `mFlyingHealth`. The port sends the original 40 through regular native `TakeDamage`, retaining difficulty/resistance modifiers and the native inlined armor decrement/pop path. A synchronous, generation-checked scope discards only the downstream helmet/body overflow of a hit that started with positive flying armor, matching the source first-armor override. Recursive damage calls have their own depth and retain their normal damage. No health is healed back and no armor is forced to zero. Native `TakeFlyingDamage` also exists, but calling it directly would bypass the receiver modifiers attached to `TakeDamage`; the native method inlines that same armor logic. Once flying armor is zero, a later hit deals ordinary body damage even during the native popping animation. Native `EffectedByDamage` categorizes `BalloonPopping` as OffGround before checking Flying (0x6f1496 and 0x6f15c9). Torchweed adds OffGround only for that exact balloon phase; other off-ground target classes and the helper ground filter remain unchanged. This prevents the target filter from silently dropping the follow-up pulse. Source `Fall` immediately clears armor and changes status; there is no source body-invulnerability period until the visual animation ends. Independent metadata and executable evidence is in `source-review/`.

The port uses saved `mLaunchCounter` for this fire timer. One second is derived from native `Il2CppReloaded.Constants.Gameplay.TIME_FOR_UPDATE`, currently 1/120 second, so one fire interval is 120 board ticks. The reviewer measured native Plant countdowns advancing once per board tick at 120 Hz; there is no separate 100 Hz plant clock. The new fire ability therefore does not reuse legacy 100-tick counters. Roasted Squash retains native Replanted behavior. The new Roasted Spikeweed instead uses its own source-timed clock as described below. The compact native timing trace is archived in `source-review/native-clock.json`. The original imported body supplies the fire animation; simulation ticks alone cause damage. Small fire peas reuse the existing `fusion:low-fire` registration, imported small flame model and native fireball impact/splash. Ignited nuts reuse the existing saved rolling-nut marker, hit history and original fire-nut graphics. Fire contact uses native FirePea Foley.

## Roasted Spikeweed

The original 1155 is an ordinary low Spikeweed with 300 health and 20 damage per second, without heat application and without any fusion recipe. Native Spikeweed owns low targeting, terrain, health and save/load; the new helper owns source-timed attack and vehicle events. The marker pair Spikeweed/Cobcannon is stable and distinct from Torchweed's Spikeweed/Torchwood. Its source `Caltrop.Update` attack check runs every 1±0.1 seconds, including checks with no targets. The port samples the same uniform jitter in seconds and quantizes once against native `TIME_FOR_UPDATE`, giving 108–132 ticks at 120 Hz. A successful check starts the original 2/3-second attack clip (80 ticks). Exactly one `AnimAttack` event at 1/3 second (40 ticks) deals 20 contact damage without heat. The official clip event and jitter constants are archived in `caltrop-clip-events.json` and `caltrop-interval-constants.json`. Vehicle contact requests the same clip immediately; its event first destroys vehicles and then completes ordinary damage before consuming the helper. Saved `mLaunchCounter` and `mStartRow` own the independent check and clip phases. Native Spikeweed attacks are suppressed only for this new helper; existing plants are unchanged. It does not qualify as a vanilla Spikeweed ingredient. It is not one of the four Roasted forms accepted by the original Ice Pot conversion method.

## Fertilizer and lifetime

`Fertilize.Upgrade` (0x682810, common branch around 0x682b4d) heals and caps the separate positive attribute timer at source offset 0x1d8. It does not cap Torchweed's attack countdown at 0x1d0, does not change either plant's conversion count, and has no special evolution case for 1059, 1061 or 1155. The port therefore heals these plants without advancing fire, generating a summon or upgrading Roasted Spikeweed to Spikerock.

Native fields own both charges, fire countdown and identities. No gameplay dictionary or animation callback adds an unsaved clock. Pause, carrying, death, board teardown and native object generations use the existing activity/index guards. Summoning temporarily clears the seed transaction context and restores it in `finally`; it consumes no player seed or Sun. Roasted plants cannot match vanilla ingredient identities. Fusion transactions continue to reserve results before consuming ingredients and retain support/pumpkin layers through the existing service.

## Verification

Build and offline checks are recorded separately from live evidence in [validation.md](validation.md). The actual TorchFusions, TorchweedFusions, RollingProjectiles and ChargeTorchFusions source is compiled together into the offline suite, which passes 3084 checks. It checks distinct thresholds, full-row behavior, one-time crossing, source232 rejection, actual rolling movement, pause, hostile targets, ground/air contact and transaction-context restoration. Weed tests pass 116 assertions, including that the summon retains 20 damage and cannot act as a vanilla ingredient. These doubles do not establish live graphics, projectile impacts or engine behavior. Independent live rolling verification passed on the frozen 22.3.4 candidate, including fire graphics, exact damage, piercing, second-stump rejection and native Torchwood/Hot Potato regression. The independent reviewer completed the full task matrix on the frozen candidates, and the orchestrator explicitly approved PASS. The two completed recipe rows have therefore been removed from `fusionen.txt`, leaving 284 open recipes with 226 result plants. The consolidated review and test limits are recorded in [validation.md](validation.md) and `runtime-checkpoints/final-review.md`. A persistent player-save roundtrip was not performed; identity and saved-counter ownership were reviewed statically. The Catapult retest passed on a clean row after a previous Zamboni ice road was identified as fixture contamination; no gameplay change followed that observation.
