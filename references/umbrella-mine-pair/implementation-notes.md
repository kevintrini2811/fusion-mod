# Bombrella and Levitater

REA 6.10.1 and Ghidra 12.2 inspected PvZ Fusion English 4.0.5.2. The source GameAssembly SHA-256 is `48096b917ee6aaf6e35c95c98e666a4399a9e0c3d5adbab727cee941eccf2d42`. The raw receipts are under `../.test-runs/task1-20261120-pair-07` from the project root.

Bombrella (`CherryUmbrella`, 1207) uses the native Umbrella identity with the Cherry Bomb marker. Both source recipe orders are available. The native Umbrella handles bungees and basketballs. On ground contact, the shared umbrella routine selects zombies in its own lane. `CherryUmbrella.BlockEffect` at RVA `0x49b320` calls the zombie knockback before the small cherry explosion. The port pushes two source units, or three with Umbrella Pot support, then creates a 300-damage blast with a 1.5-source-unit radius around the pushed zombie. Mode 2 reads the constructor's radius at `0x9d2263`, confirmed in `rea-r07-small-blast-instructions.json` and `rea-r07-small-blast-Constructor.json`. The plant has 600 health and the inherited umbrella pulse spends 100, 150 or 250 health according to target count. The model and portrait reuse Umbrella Husk, with a warm tint on the model.

The saved `mTargetX` field holds up to 100 charges. Actual healing on a Cherry-nut or Cherry Pumpkin within one row and one column supplies one charge. Cherry explosions and Cherry Pot healing share the existing recipients and health writers. The same 3×3 coverage spends two charges to cancel a bite, enemy projectile hit or direct combat removal in the existing damage scopes. Source enemy cherry projectiles belong to source-only zombie variants.

Levitater (`BloverMine`, 1426) uses the existing Blover air slot and both source recipe orders. `PotatoMine.OnTriggerStay2D` at `0x486d90` marks same-row contact before exploding. `BloverMine.OnPlantDeath` at `0x491080` checks that contact flag, matching cell and zero cooldown before recreating the same mine type and setting 15 seconds. The Replanted port observes native Potato Mine ability completion and managed contact detonation, then replants the same identity through normal placement. The flight layer remains available above the replanted mine. The saved launch counter tracks the successful replacement in source ticks. The view reuses the armed Peatato Mine body and its portrait.
