Ordinary Peas become Snow Peas. Ordinary Snow Peas become Extreme Snow Peas with twice the damage and the 20-point buildup in `Bullet_extremeSnowPea.HitZombie` at `0x7058b0`. The live native IceTorch candidates are regular Peas, Snow Peas, Fire Peas and the orange flame from Scorchwood. Source water and spruce-leaf projectile types and Pumpkin Brazier are outside the available roster. The plant view reuses Scorchwood's model and portrait with a cool body tint.

Doom-pult (`DoomMelon`, 1408) fuses Doom-shroom and Melon-pult in both orders. `Bullet_doomMelon.HitLand` at RVA `0x703750` chooses `BoardAction.SetDoom` on intact ground. The main blast deals 1,800 damage and creates an ember crater. Water and existing craters take the smaller `AoeDamage.BigBomb` path, which divides damage by 18 and expands from 1.5 to two source units when Melon Pot support is present. The shared lob solver supplies the trajectory. The landing code keeps the shot alive across contacts in flight and commits one blast when it reaches the impact height. The crater uses the native lifetime and placement rules. `Crater.FixedUpdate` at `0x9e8dc0` applies normal ember within a half-unit circle. The port uses the existing ember record and leaves the ultimate corpse-explosion flag under its own source. The plant and projectile reuse Melon Morph's resources.

The Replanted port uses regular ember behavior.

The plant identities pair `Torchwood` with `Iceshroom` and `Melonpult` with `Doomshroom` in existing save fields. The crater and ember use native grid-item records. In-flight Doom Melons and Polar shots follow the shared load rule that reinitializes marked Fusion projectiles as ordinary Peas. The main explosion uses Replanted’s Doom particle and explosion foley.
