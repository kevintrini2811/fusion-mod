# Lumos Angler and Starglow

PvZ Fusion English 4.0.5.2 `GameAssembly.dll` was analyzed with REA 6.10.1 and Ghidra 12.2. Its SHA-256 is `48096b917ee6aaf6e35c95c98e666a4399a9e0c3d5adbab727cee941eccf2d42`. Raw evidence is in `../.test-runs/task1-20261120-pair-05` relative to the project root.

- Lumos Angler (`LanternChomper`, 1241) merges Chomper and Plantern in both orders. `Chomp` at RVA `0x4becd0` sets digestion from the light present at the chomp. `SwallowEvent` at `0x4bed40` checks the full lane before looking for one eligible zombie in a three-unit circle. It accepts a neighboring lane with matching terrain and excludes gargantuars, drivers and zombies already changing lanes. Replanted keeps the native bite, chew and swallow states. It samples light on a successful bite, transfers the saved value when digestion starts, and pulls on swallow completion. Replanted native `SetRow` changes the lane and render order. Native position updates move the body toward its new lane. Fertilizer caps the outstanding digestion timer at 0.5 seconds.
- Starglow (`LanternStarFruit`, 1081) merges Plantern and Starfruit in both orders. `PlantShootUpdate` at RVA `0x563a80` resets the shot check on each interval, with 0.95–1.05 variation. `SearchZombie` at `0x563be0` starts with the ordinary star rays and uses a forward same-lane search at light 1. At light 2 an ordinary lawn no longer needs a target. `SetBullet` at `0x563e00` selects the lit projectile, with advanced status at light 3. `StarFruit.Shoot1` at `0x578e60` fires five stars. `Bullet_superStar.Update` at `0x82d8c0` leaves the normal directions in place for 0.3 seconds, then switches to tracking. Advanced status skips the same-lane filter. `Bullet_star.HitZombie` at `0x82a570` bypasses shields only in the original leftward mode. `Bullet.Update` at `0x5f3660` retires projectiles after 20 seconds. The port keeps the tracking lane and target in the native projectile fields during play. Fusion's existing load normalization converts marked projectiles in flight to ordinary Peas.

Replanted collision rectangles and movement replace the source Unity colliders and coordinates. The plants use existing Solar Chomper and Winter Starfruit models and portraits. Starglow uses the existing plain star projectile model, the native star impact particle and the native armor-aware impact sound. Its model brightens from light level 2.
